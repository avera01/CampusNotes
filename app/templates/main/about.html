About CampusNotes

CampusNotes is a regional study resource repository built for students at smaller and regional universities — starting with Nagaland University and its affiliated colleges.

Why we built this

National ed-tech platforms are built around a handful of large universities' syllabi, which leaves students at regional institutions without curriculum- specific notes, previous year question papers, or syllabus documents. In practice, students end up sharing scanned notes informally through WhatsApp groups — useful in the moment, but impossible to search, browse by subject, or trust once the group chat moves on.

What CampusNotes does

  • Organizes study material by University → Course → Semester → Subject, so you can find exactly what your syllabus covers.
  • Lets any student upload notes, previous year question papers, or syllabus documents, with their name attached so contributors build a track record.
  • Uses admin verification to mark trustworthy uploads with a "Verified" badge.
  • Supports keyword search and filtering by course/semester, plus sorting by recency, downloads, or verification status.

Who's behind it

CampusNotes is an independent, community-driven project — not affiliated with Nagaland University or any other institution. It's built and maintained on a small scale, with the goal of making it easy to extend to more universities and colleges over time.

Have notes to share, or found something out of date? Log in and upload a resource, or reach out to an admin to get it verified.
